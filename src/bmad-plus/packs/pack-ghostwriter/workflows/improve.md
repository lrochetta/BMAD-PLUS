# Improve

Read the [editorial contract](../shared/editorial-contract.md). Use `ghostwriter-edit`. Where Huldah has already supplied a structural review, start from those findings and source references.

## Inputs

For a story whose author asks to compare narrative methods, the [narrative council](../workshops/council/README.md) can supply competing structural readings. Keep the author's chosen direction with the brief before revising scenes.

Use the draft, intended reader task, approved edit depth, sources, required sections and output format. For a channel change, identify the new reader and the available space. Keep a comparison copy of the complete draft.

## Procedure

1. Trace one realistic reader through the draft. Map where they first encounter each prerequisite, decision, instruction, exception and source. Note the point where understanding breaks down.
2. Make the smallest outline change that solves that problem. Move a prerequisite ahead of the dependent step. Merge repeated explanation while retaining unique exceptions and references. Give important unresolved gaps their own working note.
3. Rewrite within the approved depth. Add explanation only from authorized material. Preserve the owner of each action, temporal sequence, evidence and degree of certainty. In a channel adaptation, explicitly account for material that cannot fit.
4. For long or multi-author texts, track the destination of every selected section. Read across each join for changed terminology, missing pronoun antecedents and contradictions introduced by reordering. Return conflicting technical advice to the subject owner.
5. Read the final text from the recipient's perspective. Check the updated order, calls to action and references against the original. If only stylistic work was authorized, report the structural recommendation separately.

## Delivery

Give the revised text in the requested format. Identify material moves, source-supported additions and open questions when a report was requested or the reader needs that context. Mark omitted or unreviewed sections in the handoff.
