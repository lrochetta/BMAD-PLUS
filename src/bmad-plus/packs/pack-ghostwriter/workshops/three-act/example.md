# The blue curtain

An original outline for trying the workshop. The author wants a quiet ending. The reason the cinema is closing must remain unknown.

**S1.** On the cinema's last afternoon, projectionist Ada hems the blue curtain. Her brother Noé has brought a school film they made together at twelve. She threads the reel. A customer asks what is showing tonight. “Nothing,” says Ada.

**S2.** The reel stops at a splice. The cut ends lie on the inspection bench. Noé wants to see the missing scene. Ada turns off the bench lamp and gives him the motor key.

**S3.** Noé unlocks the booth. The audience hears children counting backward on the soundtrack. Ada pulls the curtain closed before the first image. Noé holds its loose hem.

**Author's question.** I want the final gesture to feel chosen. Is the necessary choice visible soon enough? Keep the siblings' history ambiguous.
