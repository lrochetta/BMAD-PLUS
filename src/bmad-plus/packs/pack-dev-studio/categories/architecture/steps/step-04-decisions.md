# Architecture compatibility guide — record consequential decisions

This retained path corresponds to procedure step 4 of
[create-architecture](../create-architecture.md). Follow the common
[execution guide](../../../shared/execution.md).

For each consequential choice, state the problem, constraints, credible options,
selected approach, tradeoffs and evidence. Explain when the choice should be
revisited. Routine settled choices do not need invented alternatives.

Resolve an essential unknown through available source inspection or a bounded
authorized experiment. Otherwise mark the affected decision provisional and name
the evidence that would resolve it. Record performed experiments separately from
proposed tests.

Maintain stable decision IDs. On revision, preserve the prior decision and explain
its replacement. This part is complete when each meaningful choice is justified
or explicitly provisional; continue the main procedure at its first unfinished
part rather than loading another legacy step automatically.
