# Localize for a specific audience

Open the Ghostwriter pack beside the installed skills. Read its [editorial contract](../pack-ghostwriter/shared/editorial-contract.md). The [localize workflow](../pack-ghostwriter/workflows/localize.md) describes the full task. In a source checkout these resources live in `../../packs/pack-ghostwriter/` under the same relative names. If a required resource is missing, identify its path before proceeding.

1. Establish the source and target locale, audience, channel, available space and preferred form of address. Reuse the approved glossary and voice for that market. Resolve ambiguous dates or material regional terms with the supplied context or the author.
2. Inventory claims, instructions, quotations, product names, amounts, units, deadlines, exceptions, markup, links and placeholders before drafting. Identify which text fields can change. In structured resources, keep keys, escaping, nesting and variable bindings intact.
3. Write a fluent target-language passage around the same reader task. Adjust formality, idiom and unambiguous number or date presentation according to the target locale. Preserve the actual values, currency, units and time zone. A new local guarantee, exchange rate or legal interpretation requires separate authority and evidence.
4. Compare source and target side by side, proposition by proposition. Check who acts, when, whether the action is optional, what it costs and which exception applies. Confirm the placeholders remain in the correct clauses at every plural or conditional branch. Re-read the target in isolation for clarity and fit.
5. Return the localized copy and any material questions separately. When the source is ambiguous and the choice could change an obligation, retain the ambiguity or identify the passage that needs clarification before use.

In a billing message, the English “You may request up to EUR 20 per order” can become “Você pode solicitar até 20 EUR por pedido” in Brazilian Portuguese. Preserve the optional request, the EUR currency, the upper limit and its per-order scope.
