# Verify an editorial change

Open the Ghostwriter pack beside the installed skills. Read its [editorial contract](../pack-ghostwriter/shared/editorial-contract.md). The [agent-output-review workflow](../pack-ghostwriter/workflows/agent-output-review.md) describes the full task. In a source checkout these resources live in `../../packs/pack-ghostwriter/` under the same relative names. If a required resource is missing, identify its path before proceeding.

1. Obtain the brief, source, candidate, approved profile and scope. Inspect the actual versions that will be delivered. Identify the run and attempt if supplied by the controller. List the selected sections or files and read all of them, including transitions. Record any missing input or unread unit.
2. Check protected material with a comparison appropriate to the format. Inspect exact quotations, commands, code, link destinations, keys, variables and markup. For localized quantities and dates compare their values, currency, units and time zone. Record which checks ran and their evidence.
3. Match consequential claims in both directions. Look for new commitments, lost exceptions, swapped actors, changed comparisons and substitutions of certainty for possibility. Anchor each finding to a location in the source and the corresponding location or omission in the candidate. Explain its effect on the reader.
4. Check factual support only to the depth the brief authorizes. Identify each source actually inspected and mark material unsupported assertions or conflicts. Assess usefulness, clarity, naturalness and fit to the chosen voice separately. Preserving a sentence faithfully leaves its truth an independent question.
5. Return the review with coverage, version references, evidence and findings by dimension. Keep the evidence of mechanical checks separate from semantic judgment. Report an interrupted, partial or stale review as incomplete. Recheck changed output. In an orchestrated run, leave acceptance to the controller and supply its expected report format. A self-review cannot satisfy a policy requiring a separate reviewer.

If an input reads “Overnight delivery may be available for orders under 5 kg” and the candidate reads “Orders under 5 kg arrive overnight,” record the clause pair and the unsupported guarantee. An unchanged number and unit would pass a numeric check.
